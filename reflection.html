Reflection Letter

Dear Educational Technology Faculty,

My name is Claudia Casso, and I am pursuing a Master of Education in Educational Technology at UTRGV, with an expected graduation date of Fall 2027. I earned a Bachelor of Arts in Theatre Arts with minors in Education and Psychology from the University of the Incarnate Word. My professional experience includes work as a theatre arts instructor, youth development instructor, performing arts program coordinator, theatre director, and education and community program specialist. Through these roles, I have supported children and adolescents as they strengthen their creativity, confidence, communication, and teamwork skills.

My current course is EDTC 6320: Instructional Technology. As I begin my graduate studies, I want to connect my background in education and the performing arts with instructional design and technology. I am interested in how thoughtfully designed learning experiences can encourage creative expression while remaining accessible and meaningful for diverse learners. In educational and community settings, I hope to apply these ideas to the development of programs, instructional materials, and technology-supported learning opportunities.

After graduation, my goal is to work in a role where I can bring these interests together to support learners. I look forward to exchanging ideas with classmates, learning from their different experiences, and building professional connections. This portfolio introduces the experience and aspirations I bring to that journey.

Sincerely,
Claudia Casso
