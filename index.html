Welcome to My e-Portfolio

About Me

Hello everyone! I am Claudia Casso, 43, from San Antonio, Texas. I am pursuing a Master of Education in Educational Technology at UTRGV, expecting to graduate in Fall 2027. My background combines education, psychology, and the performing arts, including experience supporting children and adolescents through creative learning and community programs.

Purpose

This e-Portfolio introduces my professional background, educational interests, and aspirations in educational technology. It is a living record of my development throughout graduate study. My goal is to connect my experience in the performing arts with instructional design and technology to support engaging, accessible, meaningful experiences for diverse learners everywhere.

Organization

The Reflection Letter introduces my goals. The Matrix records the status of project evidence and standards alignment. Courses Taken highlights my coursework in EDTC 6320: Instructional Technology. Leadership describes my professional experience. Contact Me identifies my professional background, while Profile provides a fuller biography and my career interests.

How to Review

Begin with my Profile and Reflection Letter, then visit Courses Taken and the Matrix to review the current work. Use a current web browser on a computer, tablet, or smartphone. These pages require no special equipment or browser plugins. The navigation menu appears on every page for convenient, consistent access.

My Direction

I hope to develop educational programs, instructional materials, and technology-supported learning opportunities after graduation. Theatre and creative expression remain important interests. I look forward to exchanging ideas, learning from different perspectives, and building professional connections as I bring my experience in education and the arts into this next career chapter.
