About Me

Hello everyone! My name is Claudia Casso, 43, from San Antonio, Texas. I am pursuing a Master of Education in Educational Technology at UTRGV (expected Fall 2027). My background spans education, psychology, and the performing arts, and I design learning that supports creativity and accessibility.

Purpose of the e-Portfolio

This e-Portfolio documents my learning, projects, and professional growth while I complete the Master of Education in Educational Technology. It showcases projects, reflections, and evidence that demonstrate my development in instructional design and technology integration.

Organization

The portfolio is organized into clear sections: a Reflection Letter, a Matrix linking artifacts to standards, Courses Taken with project pages, Leadership & professional development, and Contact & Profile information to help evaluators navigate my work.

How to Review the Portfolio

View this portfolio in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox) on a computer, tablet, or smartphone. No special plugins are normally required; if a specific artifact needs a player or plugin (e.g., SCORM, proprietary simulators), a note and link will be provided with that artifact.
